Join Nightcap

A private EverQuest server for friends.

  1. Get your Nightcap account from the owner and have your public IP approved.
  2. Download the Nightcap launcher.
  3. Use a dedicated copy of your complete supported Rain of Fear 2 client on Windows.
  4. Open the launcher, choose your game folder, and install the update.
  5. Press Play, keep the launcher open, and use your Nightcap account to log in.

The launcher sets up your encrypted connection. No separate VPN or in-game command is required.

The game client is obtained separately. See FRIENDS.txt in the download for prerequisites and troubleshooting.

If your Internet connection or public IP changes, contact the server owner.
