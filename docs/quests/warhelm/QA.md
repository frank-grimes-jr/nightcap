# Ashen Compact acceptance

Candidate only. Automated checks and Blender previews are not in-game acceptance.
Use a disposable test character and the paired staged client/runtime/content.

1. At level49, Drazhul and the Kael Keeper refuse access without creating a run.
   At50, accept with Shadow Knight main, then verify a held secondary SK follows
   Camil's any-slot eligibility. A non-SK can join dungeon combat but cannot claim
   the campaign helmet. Keep Camil's journal active and verify independent state.
2. Fill Tasks. Admission must leave no new allocation or transfer reservation.
   Free space for both journals and enter. Public Kael has only its foyer services;
   private Kael has35 authored static spawns, no stock giants or later-era drops.
   Verify arrival, all doors, Keeper/waypoint meshes and return to Commonlands.
3. Confirm fourteen story rows. Later interactions cannot skip prerequisites.
   Earn the Herald, standards, defense, Marshal, knights, General, Echo and altar
   in order. A player arriving after a kill gets no retrospective personal credit.
   Repeat owner-pet, grouped, dead, disconnected and over200-unit credit controls.
4. Interrupt/escape the Herald's warned warcry. Protect Veyra through three pairs
   of war souls; next waves wait for the prior wave to die and the15-second cadence.
   Guardian death, abandonment, expiry and wipe clean only that attempt and allow
   retry. Temporary actors award no ordinary, AA or leadership XP and no loot.
5. Kharzug's half-health adds appear once; evade/death removes only his owned adds.
   Move more than10units from a General/Echo mark within3seconds, or interrupt.
   Standing at the mark is hit. Switching target cannot transfer a mark. Verify
   warnings, cast animations, line of sight, enrage and safe pathing in actual Kael.
6. Remove the journal, reconnect and reset a run. Earned chapters survive; status
   restores the display. Expired or unrelated runs never lend credit. Verify solo
   and grouped completion and both Grind and Raid, including reset with corpses.
7. Finish the altar and return. Fill inventory, then Claim Helm: entitlement stays
   pending with no duplicate. Free space, claim, reconnect and claim again: exactly
   one helmet and one completed story entry. Force receipt/history write failures
   only in an isolated fixture; retry must recover. Pausing new campaigns must keep
   earned helmet claims and history available.
8. Inspect material91 on each supported Classic race/gender, all available faces,
   idle/run/attack/cast/death, show-helm toggle, zoning and reconnect. Inspect neck
   seam, horns, UVs and the24-frame mouth animation. Check material90 Camil and
   normal helmets remain unchanged. No Iksar/Luclin/Drakkin support is claimed.
9. Play the complete route with a developed SK main and record actual secondary
   classes, gear, legal AAs, pets, resources, deaths and completion time. Arena
   diagnostics exclude adds, scripted pressure and pathing; they cannot certify
   the complete challenge. Test a weaker character to establish progression and
   a strong SK/pet or caster combination to check unintended trivialization.

Record observations, client release, native hashes and character setup. Do not
mark these steps passed merely because their unit tests or map probes pass.
