Wayfarer’s Oath: The Last Lantern

Mera Vell has three reports and the same name scratched out of every one. Follow the lost company through twelve chapters, from level20 to the current level50 endgame. Begin with the Ancient Chronicler’s journal dialogue in East Commonlands; your Tasks window tracks the journey.

  1. The First Pages

    Level 20

    Read the Upper Guk, Unrest and Runnyeye entrance reports. Earn your boots.

  2. Beneath the Surface

    Level 25

    Win the field trials, then bring Tovin through an Unrest rescue memory. Earn gloves.

  3. Steel and Sorcery

    Level 30

    Recover the named accounts and let Mera compare the records. Earn your first bracer.

  4. Choose your oath

    Level 35

    Earn Guardian, Striker, Arcanist, Warden or Binder. One path opens the continuation and the oath charm; further paths are optional.

  5. A Name Beneath the Moss

    Level 38

    Recover Mera’s satchel in Lower Guk and escort her through the retreat. Earn the second bracer.

  6. What the Fire Kept

    Level 40

    Defeat the Solusek B lieutenants and defend Oren’s furnace. Earn arms and Oathbound refits.

  7. The Captain's Last Watch

    Level 42

    Hear Captain Selene’s last orders in Cazic-Thule and hold the retreat. Earn leggings.

  8. The Debt of the Living

    Level 45

    Recover the stolen roster in The Hole and bring Mera home.

  9. Where Courage Ends

    Level 46

    Release the bound names in Fear and defeat their pursuer. Earn head armor and Restored refits.

  10. The Weight of a Name

    Level 48

    Break the chosen guard in Hate and save the recorded names.

  11. A Light Carried Forward

    Level 50

    Gather the survivors in Commonlands and choose a memorial inscription.

  12. The Last Lantern

    Level 50

    Break the bindings, defend the standard and face the Hollow Witness. Return to the campfire for the chest or robe, Complete refits and Lanternbearer title.

Your journey outfit: Earn eight useful armor pieces in legal woven, leather, chain or plate fits. Oren Lanternwright handles the earned40/46/50 refits, preserving augments and custom dye. These personal pieces cannot be traded or sold; missing receipted gear is not replaced for free. The Wayfarer’s Oath charm has five earned attunements sharing a ten-minute recovery.

The private memories preserve public zones and Keeper runs. Stay with companions, survive ordered defense waves and move from the Witness’s marked ground or stun him.
