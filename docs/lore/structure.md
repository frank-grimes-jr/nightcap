# Nightcap local character notes

## Drazhul and the Ashen Compact

October24 candidate, not deployed: Drazhul challenges a Shadow Knight to master
the stolen war-fire of a renegade cult in Kael. Its standards, Veyra's binding,
oathbound knights, General Vhalek and Echo of Endless War follow Camil's personal
campaign structure. The story is original Nightcap fiction in Rallos Zek's
established giant city. [Lore](ashen-compact.md),
[quest scope](../quests/warhelm/SPEC.md).

## Camil and the Vigil of the Dawn

Deployed for live testing on October 23: Camil led civilians to safety while
Vaelen bound the fallen Order of the First Dawn to its armor. Sered guards the
memorials, Elowen holds a prayer against three waves, Halvyr commands the watch,
and the player releases the knights, Vaelen and the Unbroken Shadow before
restoring the altar. All are original Nightcap fiction. The prior multi-zone
route and proposed second spirit defense are superseded. Native Tasks preserve
personal chapters. [Quest scope](../quests/camil-chapterhouse/SPEC.md).

## Wayfarer's Oath

The owner approved **The Last Lantern** and its main/server release on November7.
Its implemented candidate follows the lost company through Classic endgame to50.
Mera Vell keeps Captain Selene Marris's seal; Oren Lanternwright repairs the
player's earned armor; Tovin returned with gaps in his account. The private
expeditions are company memories reconstructed through the journal, so living
companions can remain in the shared Commonlands camp while their past is replayed.
Selene left the crates and returned for people. The Hollow Witness carried the
ledger and sold their names to buy himself another morning. Restoring those
voices leads to an inscription choice and the campfire ledger bearing every name.
These are original Nightcap characters and events, not new EverQuest canon.

Native dialogue now preserves each encounter's enemy name and gives the records
Selene, Tovin and Mera's voices. Mera's satchel and the company roster have their
own carriers and accounts. Eight useful armor pieces share a coordinated palette;
the charm retains its five earned choices and shared ten-minute recovery. The
player's memorial inscription persists separately as flavor, never quest authority.
[Campaign](../adventure-journal/CAMPAIGN.md),
[implementation/release boundaries](../adventure-journal/release-20261107/README.md).
Full journey, rendered scenes and encounter balance remain unaccepted until dated
player QA. Do not describe this candidate as deployed without its activation receipt.

## East Commonlands tunnel residents

Owner-requested characters, September 20, 2026. These are server-local residents,
not new claims about EverQuest canon. Implementation and appearance choices:
[named residents](../economy/residents/README.md).

- Sortre Blackdragon: Dark Elf warrior in Crafted, wielding two SSoYs.
- Camil Swiftwolf: High Elf paladin in Armor of Ro, Ghoulbane and Kite Shield.
- Rekk Diver: Halfling rogue in black chain with two daggers.
- Nalin Shadowbane: High Elf paladin in Classic planar Valorium; SoulFire chosen
  as the otherwise unspecified two-handed sword.
- Glorphyndel: male High Elf paladin in full Valorium, carrying the Falchion of
  the Koada'Vie and Trueheart Shield; joins the resident campfire circle.
- Trift: level-12 Human warrior in Bronze. A goblin killed him in Butcherblock;
  he lost his corpse and repeatedly tells other travelers about it.
- Khalure Ser`Tiras: Wood Elf druid in leather and Golden Efreeti Boots; offers
  travel to era-appropriate druid rings.

Other hail lines are light implementation flavor, not approved extended
backstories. No unfinished character arcs or additional canon are implied.

## High Keep prison break — candidate, October24

The owner's raid premise is a break-in to rescue Osargen. A relief-detail bluff
fails at the gate, the keep raises the alarm, the captains try to hold the prison,
and McCabe confronts the intruders near the cells. Osargen then relies on the
player to escort him to the entrance against pursuing guards. This is Nightcap's
instance story, not a claim about a new canonical EverQuest quest. Failed escapes
return him to his cell without restoring defeated bosses. Extended backstory and
post-rescue quest chains are outside this change. See the High Keep release spec.
