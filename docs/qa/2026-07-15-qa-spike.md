## In-game QA spike (all on Tester, WAR/NEC, PoK unless noted)

**A. Caster AAs (the fix for your bug report)**
1. Log in Tester, zone once (or just log in — mask loads on connect).
2. Open AA window → Class tab. **Expect:** Warrior AAs AND `[NEC]`-prefixed Necro AAs listed. At level 1 they'll be greyed until level/point requirements are met — visible is the pass condition.

**B. Right-click scribe (#2) + spell gates (M3)**
1. Arcane Loremaster: buy `Spell: Cavorting Bones` (or any level-1 NEC spell).
2. Right-click the scroll in inventory. **Expect:** spell scribed message, scroll consumed. ("None of your classes can learn from this scroll." = fail.)
3. Buy a CLR-only scroll and right-click. **Expect:** refused (Tester holds WAR+NEC only).

**C. Spellbook / memorize — KNOWN LIMIT, do not fail QA on it**
- The spellbook window still won't open on a Warrior chassis: that is the M2 client DLL, not yet built. Releasing/re-adding classes can't change it (`pp.class_` stays truthful by design). Scribing (B) works anyway; *casting from gems* remains blocked until the DLL.
- Optional cross-check: on a **caster-base** character (e.g. Darth), adopt a second caster class and confirm you can scribe + memorize + cast the adopted class's spells from the book/gems. That exercises every M3 server gate end-to-end today.

**D. Equipment union (M3 A4)**
1. On Tester, acquire a NEC-only item (e.g. summon one: `#summonitem` a Flowing Black Silk Sash-style NEC/caster item, or any NEC-only armor).
2. Equip it. **Expect:** it equips AND stats/HP/mana change (bonuses now apply). `/who` still shows Warrior.
3. Try a CLR/PAL-only item. **Expect:** "Your class, deity and/or race may not equip that item."

**E. Vendor dupes (#9)**
1. Open each Loremaster (Arcane/Divine/Martial/Stalwart). **Expect:** no duplicate spell names. Stock counts: 776/687/184/681.

**F. #instances (#7)**
1. Hail an Instance Keeper (e.g. Befallen), say `enter`.
2. Type `#instances`. **Expect:** "Befallen (instance N) - 11h 59m remaining."
3. Type it with no instances (fresh char): **Expect:** "You have no open instances."

**G. Fizzle sanity (M3 A3)**
- On any multiclass with a low-level adopted caster spell: cast repeatedly at-level. **Expect:** normal fizzle rates (not constant fizzling). Constant fizzle = the difficulty is still reading the base class — report it.

**H. June features (close #1/#3/#4/#5 if these pass)**
1. **#3:** log in any multiclass char → yellow "Active disciplines: ..." line in chat.
2. **#5:** on a Bard (or adopt Bard), sing a song → buff icon persists permanently in song window.
3. **#1:** Instance Keeper `enter`/`status`/`reset` flow works; instance resets with fresh spawns.
