# Classic raid augment implementation

Feature candidate for Classic level 50. The 16 single-quality definitions occupy IDs 963200–963215. Public loot uses the version-zero boss identity in its source zone, a player-entitled corpse and one roll per life. Managed loot requires Raid mode, the canonical objective spawn, and at least one eligible credited member. The existing corpse envelope retains the staged loot. Grind, unrelated instances, summoned pets and restored corpses do not roll. Hate B uses its existing managed objective routing.

## Test installation and recovery

From the feature checkout run `python tools/classic_raid_augments.py --check`. Compile the common loader, world and the full zone with the matching native headers. Apply `content.sql` to the stopped test database, rebuild its item cache using the matching shared_memory executable and boot with the paired binaries. No spell file or signed client delta is required. A per-ID loader exception provides native LORE even when Items:DisableLore is active. Standard LORE excludes the shared bank from a character’s ownership check. Different trophies are collectible. A loose trophy cannot be worn. One chest-only host and one type-22 socket are required.

For withdrawal, stop new drops by reverting the zone through the normal release procedure, retain all 16 definitions for saved inventories, and keep the compatible LORE loader while any trophies are held. No player or corpse data rollback. Before any future public write, recheck all 16 IDs, SQL afterimages, the live source overlay, client LORE serialization, and the current managed Hate layout. Native build/cache verification and gameplay acceptance are separate.

## Measured balance

The [encounter study](balance/README.md) covers all 16 trophies across four level-50 builds and four bosses, with 500 legally spent AA points on each build. The original 1,104 seeded fights produced 138 comparable three-kill screens. No trophy beat the fastest of six measured Hunt choices by more than 5%. Of 203 comparable healing screens, 29 exceeded 5%. The empty-socket control accounted for most of the excess. The remaining Dreadbone/Eye result was checked in 24 fresh fights. Dreadbone and both Hunt controls won five of six attempts, and Dreadbone healed 5.58% less per second than the healing Hunt control over all attempts.

The fight timestamps exposed a capstone reuse as short as 89.10 seconds. After correcting the persistent timer, 150 targeted fights produced 44 repeat-activation intervals with a 90.35-second observed minimum. All 105 Hunt and empty controls reproduced their original results. The 45 paired capstone fights had the same win/loss outcomes. Five healing flags remain in the targeted comparison. The study records their controls and interpretation. RoF2 player QA still needs a real chest, a relog, an eligible boss corpse and native encounters in their home zones.

## Player acceptance on a test stack

1. At level 50 compare a chest with a type-22 socket to an otherwise identical one without it. Insert a trophy, replace it with another, extract it and relog. Try head armor, a loose trophy in the chest slot, pet armor and the Power Source slot. Try two identical awards, a chest already holding the same trophy, and two different trophies. Verify ordinary equipment and augment behavior with existing regression suites.
2. In public Permafrost and Nagafen’s Lair, defeat the named dragons. In managed Raid defeat the credited canonical boss with a party member in range. Repeat against Grind, the wrong NPC, an uncredited kill, a restored corpse and a fresh run. Check the extra shared item alongside ordinary loot. In Sky check the public efreeti chain and the separate final Eye. In Fear and Hate verify miniboss and final rates independently.
3. On Cazic’s Aegis, land a hostile direct hit that leaves 34% HP, compare against 35%, zero HP and a stronger active rune. Record the next hit, partial consumption and expiration at 12 seconds. Test incoming, damage shield, pet and DoT effects separately.
4. Test Heart below full health with a landed melee hit, ranged hit, gem nuke, bolt and area spell. Compare a resist, pet, proc, periodic tick, damage shield and reflected cast. Recover at most 90 actual HP once. Swap to a different capstone, zone and relog inside the 90-second deadline. Persisted timers store whole seconds, so eligibility resumes 90 to 91 seconds after activation. The focused test activates just before a clock tick and verifies the hard minimum.
5. With the Prism, spend just below and exactly 10% of each maximum while engaged, including a low-cost spell, a discipline and non-cast endurance use. Verify only the triggering pool returns 1%, up to 50 points, and neither pool earns credit during cooldown. Test swaps, leaving combat, zero-cost actions, refunds and resource exchange spells.
6. Compare the strongest compatible Hunt chest choice to each raid augment on the same level-50 character, using identical seed/gear/AA inputs and the same bosses. Include melee, hybrid, caster and pet builds, a dragon and each planar finale. Record time to kill, lowest HP, effective healing, rune absorption and time to resource exhaustion. Flag more than a 5% improvement in median kill time or sustained effective healing. Record low-HP saves separately. Native fixtures alone cannot establish solo encounter acceptance.
