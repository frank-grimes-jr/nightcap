# Classic stock raid worn and focus effects

All four qualities of each item gain the same effect. Quality progression still comes from the item's existing stats. Equip the original item or any tier to use the effect.

| Classic encounter | Stock reward | Added effect |
|---|---|---|
| Cazic-Thule, Fear | Robe of Inspiration | Mana Preservation II |
| Magi P’Tasa and other Hate sources | Apothic Robe | Extended Range II |
| Noble Dojorn, Sky | Efreeti War Shield | Improved Dodge I |
| Phinigel Autropos, Kedge Keep | Blue Crystal Staff | Improved Damage II |
| Lady Vox, Permafrost | Warhammer of Divine Grace | Improved Healing II |
| Lord Nagafen, Nagafen’s Lair | Gauntlets of Fiery Might | Improved Dodge I |

The two robes offer a choice between mana efficiency and range. The Phinigel staff rewards direct-damage casting, and the cleric hammer retains its existing attack proc. The shield and gauntlets provide the same defensive option in different slots. Wearing both uses native strongest-worn selection. Matching worn dodge also competes with an Ascended Prismatic Scale of Dodging. The +10 worn modifier is not ten percentage points of final avoidance.

Rank II focuses have full strength through spell level 44 and lose 5% of their effect per spell level thereafter. At spell level 50 their strength is 70% before native rounding. The damage and healing effects roll between their native bounds. Matching equipment focuses use the strongest eligible effect, including the existing rank IV Prismatic scales. Spells must satisfy each focus's normal cast, target and effect restrictions.

## Serpent Sight and scope

The read-only stock loot audit found 324 base items and 1,242 base/tier definitions. Twenty definitions had a worn spell, including See Invisible, Enduring Breath and Ultravision. None carried worn Serpent Sight. The Spiroc Wingblade family carried Serpent Sight as a click on all four qualities. Across the entire item database, 58 definitions carried it as a worn effect and 47 as a click. The item data does not establish what a particular client tooltip showed.

Inputs include positive version-zero stock loot routes from Fear, Hate, Sky, Phinigel, Vox and Nagafen, plus the old construct in The Hole. The Hole audit did not establish a qualifying Master Yael drop. Managed expeditions, quest turn-ins, trigger-only and unverified routes are outside this bounded drop sample. ## Player checks

Equip a Normal and an Ascended version separately at level 50. Check the item window and socket display. On the Phinigel staff and Vox hammer, compare eligible direct nukes/heals at spell levels 44 and 50, with and without the matching Prismatic IV focus. Separate crits, resists and overheal. On the hammer check the original proc independently. For Apothic Robe compare maximum cast distance on the same eligible spell. For Robe of Inspiration compare mana spent over repeated eligible casts. For the shield and gauntlets compare one then both against the same melee attacker. Check the Spiroc Wingblade click and an untouched raid item as controls.
