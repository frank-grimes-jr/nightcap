# Classic gameplay and client QA

## Plane of Fear

Clear an approach to Cazic Thule in Plane of Fear. Use a fresh spawn and a level-50 character with obtainable Classic equipment. Record the three classes, spent AA points, gear, pets, buffs and whether any remaining denizens answer his call. Confirm he is level 55 and starts with 24,000 HP. Watch for the absence of Avatar Power across the fight. Record elapsed time, remaining resources, lowest player HP, fears and the outcome. Also check the Plane of Fear Raid instance after a fresh spawn.

## Power Source training

Fully exit RoF2, update through the launcher, and log in. Put an eligible, non-Ascended item in the Power Source slot. Keep a second copy of the same item in a bag. Earn combat XP until a whole percentage changes. Inspect both items in the same zone. The training copy should show its new percentage and the bagged copy should show its ordinary name and stats. Cross a tier boundary and inspect again. Remove the training item, place it in a general slot and verify its stats and augments. Camp and return to check saved progress.

## Ascended drops

For the subsequent 7% release, use the [updated ordinary loot checks](../ascended-seven-20261127/README.md#verification-and-in-game-qa). The following checks describe the earlier 4% armor rollout.

Loot newly generated ordinary NPCs in Unrest or Befallen. Count eligible armor and shields separately from weapons and bags. Inspect a resulting Ascended armor piece and equip it. The current expected rate is 4% per eligible armor or shield drop with an Ascended variant. Weapons and bags remain at 1%. Existing corpses can retain previously rolled items. A short run can produce zero Ascended pieces.

## Tunnel Recall

Reconnect on an existing character in standard RoF2. The primary Cairnwright case requires the privately staged Hailstone preview `2026112702`. Public preview `2026112202` can still hide the entry on that class. In the AA window, open General and include owned abilities. Look for **Ancient Attunement**. Make its hotkey, cast from outside East Commonlands and confirm arrival at the tunnel. RoF2 labels the hotkey **Nightcap Recall**. Reconnect twice and check that the rank stays owned without a second grant. Check the normal ten-minute reuse and that the character's bind point stays intact.

## Imitate Death

Use a character with Monk and earned Imitate Death. In East Commonlands, create the AA hotkey and use it in combat. Check the feign outcome and the button appearance. Click again while down, then stand and use it immediately. The standard stance and casting checks still apply. The button should recover its ready appearance without zoning. Put Purify Body on cooldown and repeat Imitate Death. Confirm Purify Body keeps its own timer. Repeat with Monk in a secondary slot and after a relog.
