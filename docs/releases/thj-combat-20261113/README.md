## Bow validation

At level 50 and 100 heroic DEX, average damage on landed noncritical arrows rose from 29.65 to 34.21 without archery damage AAs, and from 69.30 to 78.41 with Archery Mastery III and Bow Mastery I (20 points). The same 4,740 of 8,192 arrows landed per normal configuration. The level-51 stationary-target comparison showed an independent held-Ranger gate defect. Delayed/immediate arrows, Headshot replacement, throwing and spell archery have separate controls. Extending the last slope of 0.06 percentage points per point above 300 heroic DEX is a Nightcap calibration. [Bow measurements](../../combat/thj-bows/MEASUREMENTS.md) · [Bow main PR](https://github.com/frank-grimes-jr/nightcap/pull/448).

## Backstab validation

The skill gate, target eligibility, damage and hate now agree on the eligible piercer. The controlled level-50, skill-200 comparison used 7 and 18 Backstab damage. The old skill base was 42 or 108 depending on the primary hand. The corrected skill base was 108 in either order. One eligible secondary also produced 108. Explicit spell damage, primary ties, elemental resistance, legal AA extra strikes and the skill timer have separate checks. [Backstab player checks](../../combat/thj-backstab/QA.md) · [Backstab main PR](https://github.com/frank-grimes-jr/nightcap/pull/451).
