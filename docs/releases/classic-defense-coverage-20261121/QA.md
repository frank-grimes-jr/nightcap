# Exact in-game QA — Classic focus and defense

Acceptance is OPEN. Run these steps against the promoted test release; repeat representative encounters after production activation. Do not equate automated diagnostics with owner acceptance.

1. Reconnect. Inspect Normal and Ascended Onyx Earring, Stone Circlet, Molten Cloak, Valorium Gauntlets, Golden Efreeti Turban and Shiverback-hide Boots. Their new effects should match the policy and have identical rank across qualities. Existing stats/clicks/procs and custom items must remain unchanged.
2. Level20 PAL/WAR: obtain the early items from Blackburrow/Crushbone/Najena, then fight a dry bone skeleton in Unrest. Compare Minor and Light Healing with haste equipped/removed; healing must not decrease. Watch real interrupt/stun messages rather than assuming focus grants immunity.
3. Level35 PAL/WAR and a priest build: use Molten Cloak, Ring of Slime and Obsidian Ring against a wan ghoul knight in Lower Guk. Compare heal landing, cast duration and mana consumption over repeated equivalent pulls. HasteII must speed Light Healing.
4. Level50 PAL/WAR: use Valorium Chestplate/Gauntlets/Bracers, Trueheart Shield and Golden Efreeti Turban. Fight a fire giant warrior in Nagafen's Lair, then a supported Vox encounter in Permafrost. Repeat with legal Combat Agility, Combat Stability, Physical Enhancement and Shield Block ranks recorded. Greater Healing strength must remain intact. Record combat duration, damage, mana, interrupts and deaths, including bash/stuns and AE phases absent from the diagnostic.
5. Equip one Dodging scale, then another compatible worn DodgeI source (Shawl of Protection or Thick Banded Belt). The strongest item bonus remains10; duplicate sources must not add another10. Remove the scale and confirm the gear still supplies10. Check front/rear melee and ranged behavior.
6. Monk: compare Shiverback-hide Boots on/off against a frontal attacker in Hate. Confirm ordinary block remains skill-gated and the bonus does not grant parry to a class without that skill. PureINT caster: compare Cryosilk Pantaloons or caster robe DodgeI. Do not expect every low-skill build to gain a visible roll change because native rounding remains.
7. During combat, compare Stone Circlet/Savant's Cap/Golden Efreeti Turban (+1/+2/+3HP) and Soft Leather Shoes/Golden Efreeti Boots/Apothic or Insidious boots (+1/+2/+3mana). Match all other equipment and buffs. Existing item caps remain30HPregen/15manaregen; rest recovery can mask the difference. New effects cannot exceed+3HP/+3mana per tick because each uses one equipment slot.
8. Verify stock source routes and retained drops in Kedge, Fear, Hate and Sky; loot/respawn probabilities were not changed. Check a custom quest reward and raid augment for unchanged stats, effects, sockets and handling.

Rollback: stop admissions, wait for zero players, cleanly stop the whole runtime, take a fresh backup, validate full current rows and apply the owned inverse effect changes. Rebuild items with the unchanged paired loader and restart/verify before opening. Never restore a stale player inventory or database snapshot. Do not kill individual production zones.
