# Classic equipment and Temple access QA

Candidate; ordinary player acceptance remains open. Use a non-GM level50 character on the test environment. Reconnect after the paired item cache and complete world/zone restart; existing copies should display the new effects. No client download or spell/AA rank changes.

1. From Lavastorm enter the Temple of Solusek Ro with GM OFF. Entry must succeed. Try an unmodified Kunark-only zone: it must remain era-blocked. Confirm Searfire, both undead knights, Walthin and Cryssia are present. GM travel is not evidence of access.
2. With sufficient faction, tell Lord Searfire “bar of the alloy”: receive Sol Cauldron17977. With insufficient faction, receive no cauldron. Obtain the unchanged Soil of Underfoot hand-in from Priestess Ghalea in North Kaladim (four Fairy Dust), and combine the cauldron's Ronium recipe9679 with two enchanted platinum bars, soil, melatite, Mistmoore granite and Sand of Ro. Wrong/missing ingredients must fail; recipe contents and outputs remain unchanged.
3. Complete one Ro mold hand-in in Rathe Mountains, then its unchanged mold+Ronium forge recipe. Verify the resulting Ro piece has the listed effect. Repeat representative Lambent/Darkforge armor hand-ins: rewards remain Ascended, all required items and faction conditions still apply. Do not use an epic final for this test.
4. Craft Fine Plate Gauntlets21010, a permitted racial armor piece and an enchanted focus jewel (e.g. Platinum Star Ruby Veil14683). Verify the recipe still produces its existing quality and that the unenchanted/statless duplicate receives no effect. Confirm class/race/deity restrictions still apply.
5. On Paladin/Warrior compare the same normal Fine Plate or Ascended Ro loadout, healing with Light Healing and Greater Healing against SolB fire giants. Healing must not weaken; valid spell haste and mana preservation must apply within their stated spell limits. Observe interrupt pressure; native automation does not simulate full bash/AI behavior.
6. Compare new10%/20% gloves with no haste, then with a stronger existing haste item. They should improve the former and not add another20% to the latter. New shoulder ATK is at most15 additional across the full loadout.
7. On appropriate caster classes compare direct damage, rain damage, DoT ticks and lifetaps with the listed damage/affliction jewelry. Test a level49 Wizard spell with rankII versus III. Duplicate item focuses must not add; an existing stronger Prismatic focus must retain its strength. Repeat with allowed offensive AAs.
8. Equip the current full Healing/Magic/Dodging augment loadout and raid gear. Healing, HP, mana and base spell-damage stats must remain unchanged; Dodging scale and gear share strongest dodge10. Confirm normal inventory socket/type/restriction checks and symmetric removal still work.

Record character classes/race/deity, item IDs/quality, actual AA ranks/points, buffs, spell IDs, zone/NPC, before/after cast/damage/heal observations and any failure. Native formula samples and stationary timed attacks do not replace this player QA.
