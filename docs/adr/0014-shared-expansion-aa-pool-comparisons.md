# 0014: Compare shared expansion AA caps with native class eligibility

Date: 2026-09-12. Status: diagnostic policy candidates; no deployment decision.

## Context

Nightcap removes overpowered AA interactions and unlocks additional ranks and
abilities with expansion releases. Raw spent points do not identify a character's
power. Unrestricted all must not be mistaken for Classic's legal maximum, and
profile-specific lists would hide abilities available through another held class.

## Decision

Compare one shallow launch combat core and one deeper sensitivity core. Apply
each global family/rank list to every build using native class eligibility and
prerequisites. Keep equipment and controller fixed within each profile. Retain
earlier legal ranks in the deeper pool. Measure an explicit free baseline rather
than automatically including placeholder or out-of-era HP grants.

Generate requested0/100/200/500/all purchase prefixes, recording actual cost,
unused points, next rank and pool exhaustion. Identical allocations share native
fight evidence across budget aliases. The native character receives exactly its
spent points for purchase verification; unused requested budgets are audit data.
Independent verification regenerates allocations from the native catalogue and
checks exact purchases, equipment, preparation, movements and terminal outcomes.

## Consequences

The shallow option preserves longer fights with less AA power but leaves pet
Nagafen performance inconsistent. The deeper option preserves more progression
and improves melee/pet results, while retaining a large cross-role gap. Neither
is a full launch offering or a named later expansion release. Remaining utility,
support/threat abilities, obtainable BiS and stronger strategies require review.

Future enforcement belongs alongside declarative `era.json` and must cover
display, purchase eligibility and existing-rank reconciliation. Do not mutate
global class masks or deploy this diagnostic list as though those paths were
implemented. The [native study](../balance/native-aa-release-candidates.md) records
108 fights, explicit QA and limits. Main promotion still requires owner sign-off.
