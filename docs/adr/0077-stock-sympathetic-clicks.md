# 0077. Earned sympathetic spell buffs follow an expansion release policy

The Classic test candidate uses two all-class Ascended stock jewelry items with named dungeon sources. The click supplies a self buff that survives an equipment swap. This preserves the value of proc weapons and avoids tying either line to one class's cheap weapon or summoned item.

Each qualifying ordinary gem cast can trigger one separate effect capped at 5% of the largest resolved direct HP component and 48 points. An existing worn healing success has priority. The limit keeps a cheap 40-point heal from roughly doubling in a flat-48 pilot. This preserves the established healing focuses and prevents a second application of outgoing Spell Damage, Healing Amount or lifetap return. The current rank remains available as expansions open; new ranks require separate stock content, measurements and an explicit per-line release decision. See the [source, release policy and QA](../items/sympathetic-clicks/README.md).
