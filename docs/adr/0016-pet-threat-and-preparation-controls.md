# 0016: Test pet strategy separately from power tuning

Date: 2026-09-13. Status: diagnostic controls; no release tuning decision.

## Context

Removing Behemoth gear weakens the pet build substantially. A weak controller or
missing preparation can resemble a need for higher caps or stronger AAs.

## Decision

Test native Feign Death against a no-slow control with the same eight gems, then
a narrower emergency policy. Preserve native recast, aggro-memory, spell-break
and standing behavior. Require observed hate changes, not just accepted casts.
Reject these tested policies as improvements after their unsuccessful pilot;
do not extrapolate that conclusion to all skilled Feign Death play.

Separately add the legally available Resist Magic buff to the owner and all three
pets while keeping combat strategy fixed. Verify actual buff retention and full
native resource recovery. Keep AA purchases and gear identical to their paired
source-constrained cases. Resistance preparation is not combined with the failed
feign strategies in the measured comparison.

## Consequences

This distinguishes strategy/preparation omissions from power deficits without
editing live caps, NPC HP or AA access. Native spell eligibility remains separate
from RoF2 scribing/memorization QA. The study does not establish optimal pet play,
obtainable full BiS or final launch balance. See the native report for evidence,
explicit limitations and manual QA.
