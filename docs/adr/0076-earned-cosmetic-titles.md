# 0076 — Earned cosmetic titles use native selection and personal ledgers

Date: 2026-11-12. Status: implemented candidate; activation and player QA pending.

Players need permanent recognition for combat, expansion progression, skills and
adventures without introducing another source of character power.

Use the native title picker and selection authorization. Reserve stable IDs and
store per-character awards separately from mutable current skills/classes. New
general kill counters record finalized natural deaths; existing personal Keeper
and native task ledgers authorize historical achievements. Expansion completion
means a versioned, explicit dungeon checklist. Generate native requirements and
the player wiki from one reviewed catalog.

A quest-only grant cannot reliably own native death commitment or skill caps.
A second expansion mask would compete with durable full-clear history. A custom
client window adds pairing requirements without improving the selection flow.

Consequences: no client download or gameplay power change. Three additive tables
and the matching zone build are required; setup is disabled. Future catalogs must
retain issued IDs and earned awards. Full release/normal-player QA and publication
remain separate gates. Contract and recovery: docs/titles/.
