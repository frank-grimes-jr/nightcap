# 0070 — Personal Vigil ledger inside curated Chapterhouse runs

Date: 2026-10-23. Status: implemented candidate, not deployed.

The owner's Paladin helmet story must survive sessions and run resets while using
the native Tasks window. A dungeon completion alone cannot represent memorials,
defense success, knight release and a one-time reward.

Keep a separate per-character ordered campaign ledger, projected into task9800.
Reuse managed dungeon UUID/enrollment/kill receipts for participation and native
atomic item delivery for the existing helmet. Completed history follows actual
delivery. Keep Raid/Grind run journals and rewards independent. Defense attempts
are temporary and retryable; completed chapters are durable.

Chapterhouse is a scoped exception to Classic access, with an entirely authored
level50 population. Suppress stock content and redirect outward exits; do not
globally unlock Rain of Fear. Curated miniboss items and a Hunt-budget augment
provide repeat rewards without importing later-era loot.

The owner selected difficulty at maximum legal power, explicitly including
Paladin undead procs, Ghoulbane and proc/critical AAs. Tune to a level50 endgame
capstone, preserve undead bonuses, and combine measured melee pressure/HP with
interrupt, movement and guardian objectives. Lower-AA controls are not the
solo-success target. Fresh current rules/content and actual reachable purchases
are mandatory; an older synthetic fixture is insufficient. See BALANCE.md.

Alternatives considered: ordinary Tasks-only progress lacks durable recovery and
receipt control; using only a run checklist loses the personal story on expiry;
scaling stock Rain of Fear mobs leaves unbounded spells/loot/content paths.
The cost is paired schema/native/cache installation and explicit personal/run
reconciliation tests. Exact owner acceptance remains in the quest QA document.
