Character & progression

  • Three classes from level 1

    Keep your starting class and choose two different extra classes with the Ancient Chronicler in the tunnel.

  • Your whole build in view

    See Primary, Second, and Third in Inventory and with #classes. Class information refreshes when you make a choice.

  • Spells, stats, and gear for your classes

    Your build uses the best base health and mana calculations among your classes. Spell access, eligible equipment, and spell-shop choices also account for your classes. Buy and learn spells, songs, and tomes; right-click eligible spell scrolls to scribe them.

  • Clearer spell levels

    Scroll inspection removes unusable classes and shows the correct levels. The spell-selection menu also refreshes after a class change, fixing stale level-255 labels.

  • Skills earned through play

    Eligible skills unlock at their usual levels, starting at 1. Improve them through use or training. Extra-class trainers and dual wield still need checks.

  • Ability experience from level 1

    Use #aaexp 50 for a 50% combat-experience split, #aaexp off to stop, or #aaexp to check it. The ability window has a Class column; class and ability requirements still apply.

  • Extra-class changes and ability refunds

    The Chronicler can remove an extra class, refund eligible earned ability points, and adjust skill limits. Shared abilities and scribed spells remain; your remaining classes govern spell use. Try a spare character first while this is being tested.

  • A useful starting kit

    New characters begin in the tunnel with 100 platinum, a starting-class weapon, and a robe with 10 armor class and +5 to their main stat. These are one-time creation rewards.

Loot, gear & money

  • Polished, Exalted, and Ascended loot

    Custom item tiers give you stronger versions of eligible dropped equipment and bags. Ascended gear adds heroic stats.

  • Gear bonuses for casters and healers

    Item Spell Damage and Heal Amount work as percentage bonuses, including damage-over-time and healing-over-time spells. Each item-bonus total is currently capped at 250. Corrections to some tiered items are still in the works.

  • More room for core stats

    The normal core-stat cap is raised to 425, with heroic stat effects handled separately.

  • Ascended summoned equipment

    Eligible summoned gear becomes Ascended when a matching version exists.

  • More room in your bags

    Buy bags up to 40 slots, with base prices from 10 to 7,500 platinum. Merchant price adjustments, special bag restrictions, and NO TRADE rules still apply.

  • Better merchant payouts

    Merchants pay more for the loot you sell.

  • Bulk selling with #sellall

    Sell eligible items from main inventory and bags without targeting a merchant. Bags, NO TRADE, temporary, and worthless items stay. Sales are immediate and can include valuable gear. Bank anything you want to keep first.

  • A custom advanced-loot window

    Open /advloot for personal and shared drops, item inspection, corpse finding, saved Ask / Always loot / Never choices, group assignment, Need / Greed / Pass, free grab, and optional coin splitting.

  • Loot All for earned drops

    Collect eligible drops you have earned across the current zone or instance, with a result message and a separate bulk NO TRADE confirmation. Bag space, lore, cursor, and loot rights still matter. Ordinary and public-corpse looting remain range-limited.

  • Ignore and manage saved loot choices

    Use Ignore on an item to save Never for your character. Remove it from Filters to return to Ask. Turn on Use saved rules to apply those choices.

  • Winged Helm of the Dawn

    A custom Paladin helmet for the twelve original races, with both gender fits. The item and updated appearance are installed for visual testing. No acquisition quest or loot source has been added yet.

Combat, buffs & recovery

  • Beneficial buffs without timer upkeep

    Your beneficial buffs no longer expire from their normal countdown. Death, dispels, replacement, stacking rules, charges, and aura range still matter. Debuffs and hostile NPC buffs keep their timers.

  • Blessing effects that survive reconnects

    Repairs restore saved buff effects after login and stop permanent blessings expiring on the next tick. You should not need another hail or payment; player retesting continues.

  • Instant damage-over-time casts

    Your damage-over-time spells cast instantly, while keeping mana costs, target and resistance checks, and reuse limits. Direct-damage spells keep their normal cast times.

  • Melee attacks during casting

    Main-hand and offhand autoattacks can continue while you cast. Range, stuns, fear, and other control effects still apply.

  • Keep your belongings after death

    Return to your bind point with equipment, inventory, and money. You still lose beneficial buffs; other death rules are unchanged.

Pets & companions

  • Up to three permanent pets

    Each pet-summoning class you hold can keep one permanent pet, up to three in total.

  • Shared or individual pet orders

    The compact pet window shows your roster. Orders go to all pets by default; select one by name or world target to command it alone. A layout repair keeps the roster and controls clear of the buff panel.

  • Commands for Enchanter animations

    Enchanter animations accept normal pet commands without an ability unlock, including when Enchanter is an extra class.

  • Recoverable armor for each pet

    Inventory’s Pet Armor tab has eight slots per permanent class pet. Equip compatible items from an otherwise empty cursor and recover stored armor even when the pet is inactive. Charm and swarm pets are excluded; combat, distance, cursor, and item checks apply. Start with inexpensive gear during testing.

The tunnel, travel & services

  • A shared tunnel home base

    The East Commonlands tunnel brings all 16 trainer-and-spell-shop pairs together in four compact gatherings around campfires. Matching outfits help you find each pair.

  • One tradeskill workshop

    Find 22 supply merchants, six crafting stations, and the Epic Forgemaster together. Numbered shop pages help keep large spell and supply selections manageable.

  • A livelier gathering place

    Named residents share a campfire. Traders are grouped around the tunnel, with eight walkers following routes around the service areas.

  • Limited market finds and offers

    Custom tunnel traders have finite buy and sell offers, with two offers per trader. City merchants can also receive limited finds. Stock and prices remain part of balance testing.

  • Free buffs and an optional experience blessing

    Hail Matron Restora for free movement, health, armor, and regeneration buffs suited to your level. Check back every ten levels. Magus Goldwind offers the same package plus an experience blessing for 100 platinum.

  • City travel, rings, and spires

    Merrin offers ten city routes with return books. Khalure provides available Druid Rings, and Khaleid offers eight Classic Wizard Spires destinations. The Plane of Knowledge connection remains available.

  • Nightcap Recall

    Make a hotkey for Ancient Attunement in the General abilities tab. It returns you to the tunnel with a five-second cast and ten-minute reuse, preserving your bind point and other home destinations.

  • Travel to places you have discovered

    Find waypoint markers to unlock return destinations with the Waypoint Master. Discovering them all is a custom exploration quest.

  • Private dungeon runs

    Instance Keepers offer Befallen, Unrest, High Keep, Najena, Cazic-Thule, Permafrost, and Nagafen’s Lair. Hail to enter or join your group leader; use #instances for open runs and timers. Resets ask for confirmation.

Updates, interface & appearance

  • One shortcut and automatic updates

    Your usual Nightcap shortcut updates the launcher and game before Play. Keep your existing game folder; another game installation can stay running during an update.

  • The Nightcap interface loads automatically

    Class labels, the ability Class column, and custom pet and loot controls load with the game. Background interface requests no longer clutter local chat.

  • Classic character and creature appearances

    Supported older models are used where available, including original elemental colors. Creatures without an older counterpart retain their appearance.

  • Original Nightcap loading artwork

    Custom loading screens give the world its own look, including the artwork used on this page.

  • Startup and character-selection repairs

    Updates repair model archives and missing materials behind the recent Play and character-selection failures. Install through the usual launcher; no reinstall is needed.

  • Creature and pet animation repairs

    The latest updates repair shared creature animations and Enchanter weapon-pet identity. Beetle walking and death were confirmed fixed; pet motion and wider visual checks continue.

02 / Custom quests

Implemented custom quests.

Two quest lines are installed. Progression and eligibility still apply, and full player testing continues.

  1. Ascension

    Installed

    Turn an existing class epic into its Ascended version.

    • Start: Epic Forgemaster, in the tunnel’s tradeskill workshop.
    • Bring: Your supported class epic, unequipped in inventory, plus exactly 8 Essence of Ascension and 100,000 platinum.
    • Finish: Hand in the essence and platinum together. The forge consumes the original epic and grants its Ascended version.
    • Know before you go: Eligibility uses your starting class, not your extra classes. It supports the fourteen original class-epic weapon sets, excluding Beastlord and Berserker. If a Ranger carries both supported epics, both are reforged. You must already have the epic and materials; this does not provide the original epic quest.

  2. Waypoint Explorer

    Installed

    Explore the world, unlock return destinations, and earn a title.

    • Start: Approach a marker named a waypoint. The Waypoint Master in the tunnel explains your progress.
    • Goal: Discover all 72 Classic-zone waypoints by approaching their markers.
    • Rewards: Each discovery unlocks a return destination with the Waypoint Master. Finding all 72 unlocks the title the Explorer.
    • How it tracks: Discoveries are saved per character. Progress is tracked through discovery and NPC dialogue.

Adventure Journal & Epic 0.5

These eight quests have been built, but are not installed on the server. They still need in-game testing and release. The planned starting point is the Ancient Chronicler’s journal dialogue, with progress in the Tasks window.

  1. The First Pages

    Level 20

    Visit Upper Guk, Unrest, and Runnyeye to begin your journal.

  2. Beneath the Surface

    Level 25

    Defeat the froglok warden and the reclusive ghoul magus.

  3. Steel and Sorcery

    Level 30

    Defeat Battlelord Paluk and the goblin sage, then prepare for a path at level 35.

  4. Guardian: The Unbroken Oath

    Level 35

    Defeat the froglok shin lord, Lord Pickclaw, and Xicotl to earn a defensive attunement.

  5. Striker: A Decisive Hand

    Level 35

    Defeat Butler Syncall, Maid Issis, and Garton Viswin to earn a melee-damage attunement.

  6. Arcanist: The Measured Flame

    Level 35

    Defeat Battlewizard Unak, High Priest Zaharn, and Lasna Cheroon to earn a spell-damage attunement.

  7. Warden: The Last Light

    Level 35

03 / Still in the works

What isn’t finished yet.

The journal quests above and these remaining changes are kept separate from the installed list.

  • Combat and item corrections. Monk fist and item-tier spell-damage/healing fixes have a tested candidate, but are not deployed. The frozen personal casting bar is still being investigated.
  • Augment progression. The design is approved; the content has not been deployed.
  • Multiclass and balance work. Equipment restrictions, extra-class trainers, dual wield, stats, regeneration, missing ability names, and Bard movement/songs still need checks or fixes. Ability pools and rank limits are being evaluated alongside gear, buffs, pets, and encounter difficulty.
  • Player testing. Fresh installs, travel round trips, waypoint completion, loot restrictions, pets, reconnects, and new appearances still need wider testing. Installed does not mean every interaction has been accepted.

A few useful answers

Good to know.

Bring curiosity.
Keep a little room in your bags.

Can I play on my own?

Yes. Nightcap is built with solo play in mind, and friends are welcome too. Bring a thoughtful build, useful gear, and a plan—tough fights should still feel earned.

Where should I start?

You’ll begin in the East Commonlands tunnel. Hail the Ancient Chronicler for your extra classes, visit the class gatherings for spells and training, and speak to Merrin when you’re ready to travel.

We’re currently in the Classic era, with a level cap of 50.

How do I get the latest updates?

Close the game, reopen your usual Nightcap shortcut, and let it finish updating before you press Play. Your existing game folder stays in place.

Joining for the first time? Ask the server owner for an account and setup instructions.

Does “Ready to try” mean everything is finished?

It means the feature is in the game or available through an update. Bugs and balance changes are still possible. The custom quest list separates installed quests from the journal quests that haven’t been released. In the works covers other unfinished changes.

Something went wrong. What should I send?

Tell the server owner what you tried and what happened. Include:

  • Your character’s classes and level.
  • The zone, approximate time, and item, spell, ability, or NPC involved.
  • A screenshot, if it helps explain the problem.

If items, coins, loot, or refunds are missing, include the amounts before and after. Stop retrying the action until it’s been checked.

Do I get everything as soon as I log in?

Your level, classes, ability requirements, item rules, and discoveries still determine what you can use. You’ll learn spells, build skills, and unlock destinations along the way. More options may arrive as progression and balance work continue.
