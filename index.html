Three classes, one characterReady to try

Keep your starting class and choose two more from level 1. Visit the Ancient Chronicler in the tunnel to preview your choices and confirm them. Each class must be different.

Start here: Hail the Chronicler. You can see your three classes in Inventory or by typing #classes. If he asks you to camp and return after a change, follow that step.

Spells and gear for all your classesReady to try

Your spells, mana, and usable gear take all three classes into account. Spell shops show choices for your classes and levels, and scroll descriptions now show the correct level for each class that can use them.

You still need to buy and learn spells, songs, and tomes. Choosing a class won’t fill your spellbook. If an item looks usable but won’t equip, please report it.

Learn new skills as you playReady to try

Your new class skills unlock at their usual levels, starting at 1. Improve them by using them or visiting a trainer—choosing a class gives you a starting point, not instant mastery.

Trainer access for extra classes and dual wield are still being checked.

Start earning ability points at level oneReady to try

You can put combat experience toward alternate abilities from level 1. The ability window’s Class column helps you find options for your build. Your classes and each ability’s requirements still determine what you can buy.

  • #aaexp 50 — put half your combat experience toward abilities.
  • #aaexp off — turn that split off.
  • #aaexp — check your current setting.

Use these commands while the low-level experience slider is unavailable. Experience awards and missing ability names still need checking; changes to the ability pool and rank limits are part of ongoing balance work.

Change your extra classesReady to try

The Chronicler can remove an extra class. Eligible earned ability points are refunded, and skill limits adjust to your remaining classes. You keep shared abilities and scribed spells, though your remaining classes determine which spells you can use.

Try a spare character first: Class changes are still being tested. Note your classes, ability ranks, and points before the change, then check them again after zoning and logging back in.

A helpful start, with room to growReady to try

New characters begin in the tunnel with 100 platinum, a weapon for their starting class, and a robe with 10 armor class and +5 to their main stat. You receive these once, when you create the character.

  • Gear: Polished, Exalted, and Ascended tiers give you upgrades to work toward. Ascended gear adds heroic stats.
  • Summoned equipment: Eligible items become Ascended when a matching version exists.
  • Bags: Sizes reach 40 slots, with base prices from 10 to 7,500 platinum. Merchant price adjustments and item restrictions still apply.

Return, restock, set out

at the tunnel.

Your starting point for supplies, buffs, and the next trip out.

Find your trainers, spells, and suppliesReady to try

The tunnel brings your everyday stops together. All 16 classes have a trainer and spell shop, grouped into eight courts. Look for matching outfits to find each pair.

You’ll also find 22 supply merchants, six crafting stations, bags, the epic forge, blessings, restoration, and waypoint travel. Extra shops have numbered subtitles to help you find the right one.

Pick a destination and head outReady to try

Visit the travel alcove when you’re ready to leave the tunnel:

  • Merrin: Ten city routes, with nearby return books to bring you home.
  • Khalure: Druid Rings.
  • Khaleid: Eight Classic Wizard Spires destinations.

The Plane of Knowledge connection is also available. Merrin’s dialogue is working again. Travel routes, return trips, and pet travel are still being checked; you’ll only see destinations you can currently use.

A handy way back to the tunnelReady to try

Find Ancient Attunement in the General abilities tab and make a hotkey called Nightcap Recall. It brings you back to the tunnel after a five-second cast, with a ten-minute reuse timer. Your bind point and other home destinations stay as they are.

Out exploring? Discover waypoint markers to unlock more destinations through the waypoint service. Waypoint discovery and Explorer completion are still being tested.

See what the traders have foundReady to try

Tunnel traders each offer two limited buy or sell deals. Stock can run out, so there’s a reason to check back. City merchants can also turn up limited finds.

Residents, walkers, and market chatter make the tunnel feel lived in. Stock and prices are still being balanced.

Grab a blessing before you goReady to try
  • Matron Restora: Free movement, health, armor, and regeneration buffs suited to your level. Check back every ten levels for stronger versions.
  • Magus Goldwind: The same buffs plus an experience blessing for 100 platinum.

Hail either NPC and read the choices before buying. Buff combinations are still being tested for balance.

A dungeon for you and your groupReady to try

Instance Keepers offer private runs of Befallen, Unrest, High Keep, Najena, Cazic-Thule, Permafrost, and Nagafen’s Lair.

Hail the Keeper at the dungeon to enter or join your group leader. Type #instances to see your open runs and remaining time. Keepers also offer a reset, with confirmation.

Check the timer before settling in. Entry, group joining, and resets are still being tested.

Sell loot faster, for better payoutsReady to try

Merchants pay more for your loot. You can also type #sellall to sell eligible items in your main inventory and bags without targeting a merchant. It keeps bags, NO TRADE items, temporary items, and items with no value.

Bank your valuables first. This sells immediately, including sellable gear you may want to keep. It does not pick out just the junk.

Let the loot window remember your choicesReady to try

Open /advloot to manage your own loot and your group’s drops. Save an Ask, Always loot, or Never choice for each item. Groups can assign drops, roll Need / Greed / Pass, use free grab, and split coins.

You can inspect items and find their corpses from the window. You still need to be close enough, have bag space and loot rights, and follow lore and NO TRADE rules. NO TRADE pickup asks for confirmation.

Give it a try: Update the game, open /advloot, turn on Advanced loot and Apply filters, then choose Ask on a common drop. You can still loot corpses normally with advanced loot turned off.

Bring up to three pets alongReady to try

Each of your pet-summoning classes can keep one permanent pet, for a total of three. The pet window shows them together. Orders go to all of them by default; select a pet by name or target it in the world to command just that one.

Enchanter animations now follow normal pet commands without an ability unlock, including when Enchanter is an extra class. Pet behavior after zoning or reconnecting is still being checked.

Give each pet its own armorReady to try

Open Inventory’s Pet Armor tab for eight armor slots per permanent class pet. Select a pet, place a compatible item on an otherwise empty cursor, and equip it. You can inspect the loadout or remove an item to get it back—even while the pet is inactive.

This covers permanent pets; charm and swarm pets are excluded. Some special items can’t be stored, and combat, distance, or cursor checks may prevent a transfer. Start with inexpensive gear while this is being tested.

Spend less time refreshing buffsReady to try

Your beneficial buffs no longer run out just because their timer ends. Death still removes them. You can click them off, and dispels, replacement, stacking rules, charges, and aura range still apply.

Debuffs and hostile NPC buffs keep their normal timers. Strong combinations—including songs, disciplines, and defensive abilities—are part of ongoing balance testing.

Keep fighting while you castReady to try

Your damage-over-time spells cast instantly. They still cost mana and follow their usual target, resistance, and reuse rules. Direct-damage spells keep their normal cast times.

Main-hand and offhand autoattacks can continue while you cast. Range, stuns, fear, and other control effects still matter. We’re also investigating the reported frozen casting bar.

Keep your belongings when you dieReady to try

You return to your bind point with your equipment, inventory, and money. There’s no corpse run to recover your belongings.

You still lose your beneficial buffs, and other death rules are unchanged.

One shortcut keeps you up to dateReady to try

Use your usual Nightcap shortcut. It updates the launcher and game before you press Play, using the game folder you already have.

The Nightcap interface loads automatically, including class labels, the ability Class column, and pet controls. Its background requests no longer appear in local chat.

To get the latest fixes: Close the game, reopen the shortcut, and let the update finish before pressing Play.

Classic looks and Nightcap loading screensReady to try

Characters and creatures use classic appearances wherever a supported older model is available, including the original elemental colors. Other creatures keep their existing look.

You’ll also see Nightcap’s own loading artwork. The October 1 update repairs the model files behind the recent Play failure; wider appearance checks continue.

04 / Before you head out

Good to know.

Bring curiosity.
Keep a little room in your bags.

Can I play on my own?

Yes. Nightcap is built with solo play in mind, and friends are welcome too. Bring a thoughtful build, useful gear, and a plan—tough fights should still feel earned.

Where should I start?

You’ll begin in the East Commonlands tunnel. Hail the Ancient Chronicler for your extra classes, visit the class courts for spells and training, and speak to Merrin when you’re ready to travel.

We’re currently in the Classic era, with a level cap of 50.

How do I get the latest updates?

Close the game, reopen your usual Nightcap shortcut, and let it finish updating before you press Play. Your existing game folder stays in place.

Joining for the first time? Ask the server owner for an account and setup instructions.

Does “Ready to try” mean everything is finished?

It means the feature is in the game or available through an update. Bugs and balance changes are still possible. The In the works section shows what’s coming next and what we’re still checking.

Something went wrong. What should I send?

Tell the server owner what you tried and what happened. Include:

  • Your character’s classes and level.
  • The zone, approximate time, and item, spell, ability, or NPC involved.
  • A screenshot, if it helps explain the problem.

If items, coins, loot, or refunds are missing, include the amounts before and after. Stop retrying the action until it’s been checked.

Do I get everything as soon as I log in?

Your level, classes, ability requirements, item rules, and discoveries still determine what you can use. You’ll learn spells, build skills, and unlock destinations along the way. More options may arrive as progression and balance work continue.
