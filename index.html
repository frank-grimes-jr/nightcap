Spend more time adventuring and less time finding your way around. Class choices, equipment, mana, and encounter execution still matter. Better access should make a challenge possible, while leaving the achievement worth celebrating.

Your first steps

  1. Visit Plane of Knowledge. The arrival hub brings class merchants and useful services together.
  2. Meet the Ancient Chronicler. View your classes, preview a discipline, and confirm your choices.
  3. Stock up, then head out. Buy the spells, songs, or tomes appropriate to your classes and level. Prepare for the journey.

What makes Nightcap different

More ways to make a character yours.

Established features alongside the newest live testing changes. Full class coverage is still being verified.

01

Three-class characters

Keep your original class and choose up to two additional disciplines through the Ancient Chronicler, available from level 1. Combine eligible spells, skills, and AAs to shape your build. Each ability’s requirements still apply.

02

A clearer Plane of Knowledge

Sixteen class merchants now surround the arrival area, with a clear central aisle. Find your class’s spells, songs, or tomes, then visit the nearby bag, blessing, epic, and waypoint services. The new layout is available for live testing.

03

Gear worth pursuing

Polished, Exalted, and Ascended equipment extend item progression. Ascended gear includes heroic stats, with item scaling shaped by the server’s balance goals. Difficult encounters should continue to offer worthwhile rewards.

04

Progress from the beginning

AA experience is enabled from level 1 and is ready for in-game verification. Eligible skills unlock at their native levels with an initial value of 1, then improve through use or training. Choosing a discipline does not instantly master it.

05

Explore, discover, return

Ancient Attunement provides a way back to Plane of Knowledge. Discover waypoint markers while adventuring, then use the hub’s waypoint service to revisit discovered destinations. Waypoint travel and discovery remain on the live testing checklist.

06

Practical help at the hub

Buy bags from Bartholomew Packwell, seek blessings, and visit the epic service. Bags now have prices that increase with capacity. Larger containers are an investment; some containers retain restrictions on what they hold.

What to look for on your next login.

These changes are deployed for player testing. They have not yet completed in-game acceptance.

Find your bearings

The PoK arrival hub is open

Class merchants, the Chronicler, and five utility services are grouped near arrival. The old Loremaster shops have been replaced. Check the Find window, walk the new hub, and tell us about blocked paths or misplaced NPCs.

Know your build

Your classes are easier to see

Login messages and the Chronicler show Primary, Second, and Third classes, including empty slots. You can also type #classes or /say !classes. Selection and release responses immediately show saved choices.

Pack for the journey

Every bag now has a price

The 22 bags range from a base price of 10 platinum for 8 slots to 7,500 platinum for 40 slots. Your displayed quote can vary with normal merchant modifiers. These bags remain NO TRADE and cannot be sold back. Prices and container usefulness are part of testing.

Keep what you earned

AA purchases and class release have new safeguards

Purchases, earned ranks, and eligible refunds now use the updated persistence system. Releasing a discipline removes class-only abilities and refunds eligible earned points while keeping abilities shared with retained classes. Test purchases, zoning, relogging, and release on a disposable character first.

Put your build to work

Weapon, pet, and buff fixes are ready to test

Changes address damage with held-class weapons, ownership and attack commands for extra pets, and the expiration of directly activated self-AA buffs. Level-1 AA experience is also enabled. Record the result for your specific build; full coverage is still being verified.

Still in progress

Some equipment, skill, and trainer interactions remain blocked by the client. Bard movement and songs while running, unnamed AA entries, augment content, and an automatic launcher still have unfinished work. Their appearance on the roadmap is not a promise that they are available.

Before you head out

A few useful answers.

Is Nightcap intended for solo play?

Yes. Solo play is the preferred balance target, and playing with friends is welcome. Preparation, earned equipment, resources, and good execution should still matter.

What era and level cap are active?

Classic is the current progression era, with a level cap of 50. Plane of Knowledge remains the shared hub. Custom multiclass access and the current spell offering do not imply strict recreation of every original-era restriction.

How do I choose and check my classes?

Visit the Ancient Chronicler near the PoK arrival point. Both additional slots can be chosen from level 1; your original class stays the same. Preview and confirm each choice. Hail again, type #classes, or use /say !classes to check your slots. Camp to character selection and return when instructed.

Are spells and skills automatically mastered?

No. Buy eligible spells, songs, and tomes from the appropriate class merchant and learn them normally. Eligible skills begin at 1 at their native unlock levels and improve through use or optional training. Level and ability restrictions still apply.

Can I change my additional classes?

The Chronicler offers discipline release. The new release and refund behavior is in live testing, so use a disposable character to verify it before changing a valued build. Record your ranks and points before and after, then zone and relog to check persistence.

Why can’t I equip an item or use a held-class trainer?

Some combinations still encounter client restrictions. These remain open issues even when the server supports the action. Report your original class, held classes, level, item or trainer, and the exact message or blocked action.

Is there an automatic launcher or a public signup?

A local QA launcher can install its bundled patches and start the supported client. Hosted automatic updates and the public release remain in development. This site is a feature guide, not a client download or account signup service. Use the setup and access instructions provided by the server owner.

How should I report a testing result?

Include your classes and level, the zone, what you did, what happened, and what you expected. For purchases or refunds, record the exact item or AA and your before/after points or coin. Use the issue tracker to keep results together.
