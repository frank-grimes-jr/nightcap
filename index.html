Custom loot window

  • Custom loot window

    Open /advloot to see personal and shared drops together. Inspect an item, find its corpse, or leave that drop behind without changing its saved rule. Drops stay organized across pages.

  • Loot All for earned drops

    Collect eligible drops you have earned across the current zone or instance, with a result message. Formerly restricted items no longer require a NO TRADE confirmation under free trading. Bag space, lore, cursor, and loot rights still matter; ordinary and public-corpse looting remain range-limited.

  • Ignore unwanted drops

    Click Ignore beside an item to save Never for your character. Remove it from Filters to return to Ask. Turn on Use saved rules to apply your choices.

  • Saved loot preferences and filter search

    Choose Ask, Always loot, or Never for an item, with saved Need and Greed choices for shared rolls. Search the Filters list to find a saved choice. Preferences stay with that character after reconnecting.

  • Need, Greed, and Pass

    Shared loot supports timed rolls. Need takes priority over Greed, and only eligible players can win. You can pass on an item you do not want.

  • Group assignment and free grab

    The eligible loot leader can assign a shared drop to an eligible player or open it for manual free grab. Existing loot rights still apply.

  • Automatic coin collection and optional splitting

    Eligible earned coins can be collected through advanced loot. Auto Split shares them with connected group or raid members in the current zone; the looter keeps any remainder.

  • Choose how the loot window behaves

    Use Open on new loot to show the window automatically, Loot everything to collect eligible personal drops, or Unfiltered items only to focus on undecided items. Settings also control saved rules, coin splitting, and whether advanced loot is enabled. Turn it off to use ordinary corpse looting.

Multiple pets & custom pet window

  • Multiple pets on one character

    Keep up to three permanent summoned pets at once: one for each pet-summoning class you hold. Your class choices decide which companions you can bring.

  • Custom pet window

    The compact Pet Info window shows all three pets with their class, name, and health. A visible Commands: line tells you whether orders will affect one pet or all of them.

  • Control one pet or all pets

    Click a pet’s name or target your pet to select it. Targeting an enemy keeps that selection. Click All pets, click the selected name again, or clear your target to return to shared orders.

  • Window buttons and /pet commands work together

    Attack, Back off, Guard, Follow, Sit / Stand, Taunt, and other orders use the displayed selection. Your /pet commands use it too. Advanced controls still need their ability unlocks; ownership, range, and control effects still apply.

  • Health and buffs for the selected pet

    Selecting a pet updates the detailed gauges and buff display while keeping the complete roster visible. The repaired layout keeps the roster, command buttons, and buff panel clear of one another.

  • Commands for Enchanter animations

    Enchanter animations accept normal pet commands without an ability unlock, including when Enchanter is an extra class.

  • Custom Pet Armor tab

    Open Inventory → Pet Armor for eight equipment slots per permanent class pet. Previous / Next switches between loadouts. Inspect, equip, and remove compatible armor; choosing an armor loadout does not change your command selection.

  • Recover your pet’s stored equipment

    Stored armor reapplies when you summon its class pet and remains available to inspect and remove while the pet is inactive or its class has been released. Equip from an otherwise empty cursor. Temporary summoned items cannot go in these stored armor slots. Charm and swarm pets are excluded; combat, distance, cursor, and item restrictions apply. Start with inexpensive gear during testing.

  • Pets that travel with you

    Valid class pets, their buffs, and separate armor loadouts are saved across zoning and camping. After returning, commands start on All pets. Death removes summoned pets; stored armor remains recoverable. Lifecycle testing continues.

Classes, skills & progression

  • Three classes from level 1

    Keep your starting class and choose two different extra classes with the Ancient Chronicler in the tunnel.

  • Your whole build in view

    See Primary, Second, and Third in Inventory and with #classes. Class information refreshes when you make a choice.

  • Best base health and mana from your classes

    Your character uses the strongest base-health and base-mana calculations among the classes you hold. A melee starting class can gain a mana pool through a caster class.

  • Learn and cast spells from your classes

    Spell access and spell-shop choices account for your held classes. You still buy and learn spells, songs, and tomes. Right-click an eligible spell scroll to scribe it.

  • Equipment access across your classes

    Server equipment checks recognize your held classes. Some combinations still need client-side and in-game testing; off-class equipment issues remain on the testing list.

  • Clearer spell levels

    Scroll inspection removes unusable classes and shows the correct levels. The spell-selection menu also refreshes after a class change, fixing stale level-255 labels.

  • Skills earned through play

    Eligible skills unlock at their usual levels, starting at 1. Improve them through use or training. Extra-class trainers and dual wield still need checks.

  • Extra-class skills and independent Monk attacks

    Eligible skills appear in Actions → Abilities and can be assigned to hotbuttons. Held Monk characters have separate cooldowns for Kick, Round Kick, Tiger Claw, Eagle Strike, Dragon Punch/Tail Rake and Flying Kick; skill requirements and each attack’s own reuse still apply. Broader class combinations and balance need testing.

  • Two ability points per level while leveling

    Installed for live testing. At 0% AA allocation, eligible kills and quest/task rewards give full normal experience plus two AA per level-equivalent: about 98 AA from level 1 to 50 before starter points or extra grinding. Normal leveling rates stay unchanged. Existing points, purchases and partial AA progress are preserved. Small awards can round down.

  • AA controls from level 1; pause leveling when you choose

    Installed for live testing. Close the game and update through launcher Play for game 2026102301. Use the native AA +/− buttons or #aaexp 0 through #aaexp 100. At 0%, level normally with passive AA. Starting at the beginning of a level, at 50%, one full-level equivalent of eligible experience earns half a level and 11 AA. Rewards crossing a level boundary use each level’s own experience span. At 100%, leveling stops and the same experience earns 20 AA. Your percentage persists through zoning and reconnecting; off/on remain aliases for 0/100. Native gameplay acceptance is still open.

  • Class origins and recommended abilities

    The ability window identifies class origins across its four tabs. Enable Recommended beside Can Purchase for up to ten next ranks emphasizing survival, sustain and useful damage across your held classes. It starts off; clear it to browse the full catalog. Recommendations never buy abilities or grant unavailable skills.

  • Extra-class changes and ability refunds

    The Chronicler can remove an extra class, refund eligible earned ability points, and adjust skill limits. Shared abilities and scribed spells remain; your remaining classes govern spell use. Try a spare character first while this is being tested.

  • A useful starting kit

    New characters begin in the tunnel with 100 platinum, a starting-class weapon, and a robe with 10 armor class and +5 to their main stat. These are one-time creation rewards.

Custom gear, bags & selling

  • All items are player-tradable

    Trade formerly NO DROP and NO TRANSFER items, already-attuned gear, bags, their contents, and augments. Lore ownership and inventory capacity still apply. Free trading does not remove class/equipment requirements, temporary-item expiration, or Pet Armor restrictions.

  • Polished, Exalted, and Ascended loot

    Eligible dropped equipment and bags can appear in three stronger custom tiers. Their health, mana, defenses, and offensive stats scale by item family.

  • New augments and gear in 48 Classic zones

    Each eligible native enemy has a separate 0.5% chance for its zone’s augment and 0.5% chance for its new gear piece. Both can drop together, and ordinary loot remains. Each family averages one drop per 200 eligible kills; there is no guaranteed drop or pity counter. Once a family drops, its quality is Base 60%, Polished 30%, Exalted 8%, or Ascension 2%. Cities, hubs, pets, merchants and summoned copies do not provide these rewards. See the zone reward list.

  • Six armor locations for heroic augments

    Eligible Base and tiered head, arms, hands, chest, legs and feet armor gains one type-22 socket, preserving existing sockets. Zone augments add stats and heroic attributes; item level requirements still apply. At level 50, every two effective heroic INT from these socketed augments allows one more point of item Spell Damage, up to +30; heroic WIS does the same for Healing Amount. With the current 250 cap, either can reach 280. Extra cap room only helps when you have enough of the actual bonus.

  • Upgrade matching items with the right recipe

    Use exactly three identical eligible Normal/Base copies for one Polished, four Polished for one Exalted, or four Exalted for the final quality. Artisan Veyra’s reusable Reforging Box accepts eligible ordinary and zone-hunt items; #zoneforge handles zone rewards outside combat. Keep inputs unequipped and unmodified, empty your cursor, and close loot/trade/merchant windows. There is no coin fee or random failure. Dungeon rewards remain separate. Final ordinary gear is Ascended; final zone rewards are Ascension.

  • Heroic stats on Ascended equipment

    Ascended gear and Ascended epics add heroic stats on top of their improved base stats. This describes the existing equipment tiers; the new zone rewards have their own heroic stats at each quality.

  • Gear bonuses for casters and healers

    Item Spell Damage and Heal Amount work as percentage bonuses, including damage-over-time and healing-over-time spells. The base cap for each item-bonus total is 250. Eligible zone augments can raise it to 280 at level 50 when enough actual bonus is equipped. Corrections to some existing tiered items are still in the works.

  • More room for core stats

    The normal core-stat cap is raised to 425, with heroic stat effects handled separately.

  • Ascended summoned equipment

    Eligible summoned gear becomes Ascended when a matching version exists.

  • More room in your bags

    Buy bags up to 40 slots, with base prices from 10 to 7,500 platinum. Merchant price adjustments and each bag’s capacity and contents restrictions still apply. Free trading does not reduce expensive bag purchase prices.

  • Better payouts for ordinary loot

    Boosted merchant payouts remain in place for ordinary loot. Formerly NO DROP items use the fixed one-platinum payout below instead of those price modifiers.

  • One platinum for formerly NO DROP items

    Ordinary merchants and #sellall pay exactly 1 platinum per physical item from the formerly NO DROP pool. Five stack units pay 5 platinum; a charged object pays 1 platinum total. Purchase prices have a one-platinum floor. Merchant previews can differ from the actual payment. The new zone-hunt augments and gear are an exception: they have no merchant resale value.

  • Bulk selling with #sellall

    Sell eligible items from main inventory and bags without targeting a merchant. Containers stay. Ordinary temporary and zero-value items are skipped, but formerly NO DROP items—including temporary summoned rewards—now sell too. Sales are immediate and can include valuable gear. Bank anything you want to keep first.

  • Winged Helm of the Dawn

    A custom Paladin helmet for the twelve original races, with both gender fits. The item and updated appearance are installed for visual testing. No acquisition quest or loot source has been added yet.

Combat, buffs & recovery

  • Beneficial buffs without timer upkeep

    Beneficial buffs on you and your owned pets no longer expire from their normal countdown. Death, dispels, replacement, stacking rules, charges, and aura range still matter. Debuffs and hostile NPC buffs keep their timers.

  • Long-lasting beneficial Bard songs

    Beneficial Bard song effects are included in the lasting-buff changes, reducing repeated upkeep. Harmful effects such as damage, charm, and mez keep their normal rules. Movement-song behavior is still being checked.

  • Blessing effects that survive reconnects

    Repairs restore saved buff effects after login and stop permanent blessings expiring on the next tick. You should not need another hail or payment; player retesting continues.

  • Instant damage-over-time casts

    Your damage-over-time spells cast instantly, while keeping mana costs, target and resistance checks, and reuse limits. Direct-damage spells keep their normal cast times.

  • Melee attacks during casting

    Main-hand and offhand autoattacks can continue while you cast. Range, stuns, fear, and other control effects still apply.

  • Keep your experience and belongings after normal death

    Normal death no longer removes experience or levels, and ability progress remains intact. Return to your bind point with equipment, inventory, and money. You still lose beneficial buffs. Previous losses are not refunded; deliberate experience costs such as Sacrifice remain.

Tunnel life, shops & services

  • A shared tunnel home base

    The East Commonlands tunnel brings all 16 trainer-and-spell-shop pairs together in four compact gatherings around campfires. Matching outfits help you find each pair.

  • A dedicated workshop and larger material stacks

    Find 22 supply merchants, six crafting stations, and the Epic Forgemaster in the tunnel’s workshop. Existing stackable items hold at least 100 per stack; larger limits remain and equipment stays separate. Single combines and Craft All consume stacked ingredients. Haldra, Smithing Supplies III, sells Leather Padding; Mira Coinwell provides nearby banking.

  • More complete shops through numbered pages

    Large spell and tradeskill catalogs use numbered shop pages so the available stock fits into usable merchant windows.

  • A livelier gathering place

    Named residents share a campfire. Traders are grouped around the tunnel, with eight walkers following routes around the service areas.

  • Residents with local conversation

    Named residents bring occasional local conversation and auction calls to the tunnel, alongside the walking routes and campfire gatherings.

  • Finite tunnel-trader offers

    Custom tunnel traders have limited buy and sell offers, with two offers per trader. Stock can run out; prices and earning rates remain part of balance testing.

  • Limited finds at city merchants

    City merchants can receive small quantities of special finds as part of the custom market. Availability changes with the market’s stock.

  • Free buffs for your level

    Hail Matron Restora for movement, health, armor, regeneration, and mana-regeneration buffs suited to your level. Check back every ten levels for the next package.

  • XP blessing retired

    Magus Goldwind no longer charges platinum or grants the XP package. Matron Restora still offers free ordinary buffs. Existing timed XP boosts retain their remaining duration; old nonexpiring copies receive a minimal countdown on load and fade when buff timers run. Replacement earned boosts are under consideration.

Travel & private adventures

  • Ten city routes with return books

    Speak to Merrin in the tunnel for ten city destinations. Return books bring you back to the shared hub.

  • Druid Ring travel

    Khalure offers the currently available Classic Druid Rings. Only destinations ready for use are offered.

  • Wizard Spire travel

    Khaleid offers eight Classic Wizard Spire destinations from the tunnel travel area.

  • A Plane of Knowledge connection

    A dedicated travel connection keeps the Plane of Knowledge reachable alongside the tunnel’s city routes.

  • Nightcap Recall

    Make a hotkey for Ancient Attunement in the General abilities tab. It returns you to the tunnel with a five-second cast and ten-minute reuse, preserving your bind point and other home destinations.

  • Travel to places you have discovered

    Find waypoint markers to unlock return destinations with the Waypoint Master. Discovering them all is a custom exploration quest.

  • Private dungeon runs

    Instance Keepers offer Befallen, Unrest, High Keep, Najena, Cazic-Thule, Permafrost, and Nagafen’s Lair. Hail to enter or join your group leader; use #instances for open runs and timers. Resets ask for confirmation.

Launcher, interface & appearance

  • One shortcut and automatic updates

    Your usual Nightcap shortcut updates the launcher and game before Play. For the current combat and ability-window update, fully close both the game and launcher, then reopen that shortcut. Keep your existing game folder; another game installation can stay running during an update.

  • The Nightcap interface loads automatically

    Class labels, the ability Class column, and custom pet and loot controls load with the game. Background interface requests no longer clutter local chat.

  • Spellbook, spell gems, and mana for your build

    A melee starting class can use the spellbook and spell gems when your held classes provide spell access. The mana gauge follows your actual mana pool.

  • Classic character and creature appearances

    Supported older models are used where available, including original elemental colors. Creatures without an older counterpart retain their appearance. The game’s model configuration also supports opting into modern appearances; a full restart is required.

  • Ten original paintings across the loading gallery

    All 55 numbered loading screens and the fallback now use ten original Nightcap paintings, including the SoulFire Paladin and the gnome with three pets. The launcher installs the complete gallery while preserving your settings.

  • Startup and character-selection repairs

    Updates repair model archives and missing materials behind the recent Play and character-selection failures. Install through the usual launcher; no reinstall is needed.

  • Creature and pet animation repairs

    The latest updates repair shared creature animations and Enchanter weapon-pet identity. Beetle walking and death were confirmed fixed; pet motion and wider visual checks continue.

02 / Custom quests

Implemented custom quests.

Two quest lines are installed. Progression and eligibility still apply, and full player testing continues.

  1. Ascension

    Installed

    Turn an existing class epic into its Ascended version.

    • Start: Epic Forgemaster, in the tunnel’s tradeskill workshop.
    • Bring: Your supported class epic, unequipped in inventory, plus exactly 8 Essence of Ascension and 100,000 platinum.
    • Finish: Hand in the essence and platinum together. The forge consumes the original epic and grants its Ascended version.
    • Know before you go: Eligibility uses your starting class, not your extra classes. It supports the fourteen original class-epic weapon sets, excluding Beastlord and Berserker. If a Ranger carries both supported epics, both are reforged. You must already have the epic and materials; this does not provide the original epic quest.

  2. Waypoint Explorer

    Installed

    Explore the world, unlock return destinations, and earn a title.

    • Start: Approach a marker named a waypoint. The Waypoint Master in the tunnel explains your progress.
    • Goal: Discover all 72 Classic-zone waypoints by approaching their markers.
    • Rewards: Each discovery unlocks a return destination with the Waypoint Master. Finding all 72 unlocks the title the Explorer.
    • How it tracks: Discoveries are saved per character. Progress is tracked through discovery and NPC dialogue.

03 / Still in the works

Can I play on my own?

Yes. Nightcap is built with solo play in mind, and friends are welcome too. Bring a thoughtful build, useful gear, and a plan—tough fights should still feel earned.

Where should I start?

You’ll begin in the East Commonlands tunnel. Hail the Ancient Chronicler for your extra classes, visit the class gatherings for spells and training, and speak to Merrin when you’re ready to travel.

We’re currently in the Classic era, with a level cap of 50.

How do I get the latest updates?

Fully close the game and launcher, reopen your usual Nightcap shortcut, and let it finish updating before you press Play. Your existing game folder stays in place.

Joining for the first time? Ask the server owner for an account and setup instructions.

Does “installed” mean everything is finished?

It means the feature is in the game or available through an update. Bugs and balance changes are still possible. The custom quest list separates installed quests from the journal quests that haven’t been released. In the works covers other unfinished changes.

Something went wrong. What should I send?

Tell the server owner what you tried and what happened. Include:

  • Your character’s classes and level.
  • The zone, approximate time, and item, spell, ability, or NPC involved.
  • A screenshot, if it helps explain the problem.

If items, coins, loot, or refunds are missing, include the amounts before and after. Stop retrying the action until it’s been checked.

Do I get everything as soon as I log in?

Your level, classes, ability requirements, item rules, and discoveries still determine what you can use. You’ll learn spells, build skills, and unlock destinations along the way. More options may arrive as progression and balance work continue.
