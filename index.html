Major Features

🎭

Multiclass System

Adopt up to two additional class disciplines beyond your original class. Every character becomes a powerful 2-3 class hybrid starting at level 20.

🏛️

Universal Hub

All new characters start in Plane of Knowledge, regardless of race or class. A central, neutral meeting ground for all players.

⚡

Ancient Attunement

Every character gets a free AA that recalls them to PoK with a 10-minute timer. Available from level 1.

🕐

Expansion Management

Server can be locked to any Classic through Gates of Discord era. Switch expansions with one click to revisit your favorite content.

📈

AA Progression

Alternate Advancement is accessible from level 1. AA point cost tuned to feel rewarding throughout your entire leveling journey.

⚙️

GM Web Tool

Easy-to-use interface for character management, expansion control, teleportation, and server configuration. No command-line needed.

The Magic Behind Nightcap

🎭

Multiclass Disciplines

The single largest departure from vanilla EQ. Transform any character into a hybrid class powerhouse:

  • Level 20: Adopt a 2nd discipline
  • Level 50: Adopt a 3rd discipline
  • All spells, skills, and AAs from adopted classes become available
  • Revoke disciplines anytime without penalty
  • Visit The Ancient Chronicler in PoK to manage your disciplines

"Long have the ancient paths of Norrath been sealed... The Ancient Chronicler walks the forgotten roads between these disciplines."

🚪

Expansion-Gated Portals

Relive your favorite era without spoilers. Portal doors in PoK are locked to their appropriate expansion era.

  • Server locked to Classic? Kunark portals won't open
  • Switch expansions from the GM web tool
  • Instant effect across all active characters
  • GM's bypass all restrictions
  • Relive the vanilla experience, then explore everything
📊

Balanced Progression

Carefully tuned XP rates and AA costs create a rewarding leveling experience from 1 to max level.

  • XP Multiplier: 0.65x (slightly slower than live)
  • AA Cost: 50,000 XP per point (vs. 24M on live)
  • Sweet Spot: Level 30 in Lower Guk for optimal power
  • AAs feel meaningful, not grindy
  • Hybrid classes come online as you level
📚

Smart Spell Vendors

Three specialized vendors in PoK automatically adapt to the current expansion. No more searching for spells.

  • Arcane Tomes: Wizards, Necromancers, Magicians, Enchanters
  • Sacred Rites: Clerics, Druids, Shamans
  • Martial Disciplines: Paladins, Rangers, Shadow Knights, Bards, Beastlords
  • Inventory refreshes automatically with each expansion change
  • Your multiclass disciplines unlock new spell vendors
