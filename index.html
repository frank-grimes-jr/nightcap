More ways to play, with progression still earned.

Three classes from level oneDeployed

Your original class stays with you. Visit the Ancient Chronicler in the tunnel to choose two additional, distinct classes from level 1. Preview and confirm each choice; class and ability restrictions still apply.

Try it: Hail the Chronicler, open Inventory, or type #classes to see Primary, Second, and Third. Follow any camp-and-return instruction after changing classes.

Class-aware spells, shops & gearDeployed

The custom interface and server account for your held classes when handling spell access, mana, and eligible gear. Spell merchants filter for your classes and usable levels. The newest inspection fix removes unusable class entries and shows each class’s actual spell level.

You still buy and learn spells, songs, and tomes normally. Selecting a class does not fill your spellbook. Some equipment, trainer, and display combinations still need testing; report an item that looks usable but refuses to equip.

Skills that grow with your buildDeployed

Eligible class skills unlock at their native levels with a starting value of 1, then improve through use or training. Class selection refreshes your class information immediately, including when no new skill unlocks.

Choosing another class does not automatically master its skills. Additional-class trainers and dual-wield behavior remain part of player testing.

Earlier alternate-ability progressionDeployed

Alternate-ability experience is enabled from level 1. The ability window includes a Class column to make eligibility easier to understand, and earned purchases and refunds are saved with your character.

Set your split: Use #aaexp 50 to direct 50% of combat experience to abilities, #aaexp off to stop, or #aaexp to see the current setting. This works around the missing low-level allocation control.

Held-class eligibility and each ability’s requirements still apply. Experience awards and some missing ability names still need verification. Further ability-pool and rank-cap curation is balance work in progress.

Safer changes to extra classesDeployed

The Chronicler can release an additional discipline. The class change, eligible earned-ability refunds, and remaining-class skill limits are handled together. Shared ability families remain owned; scribed spells stay in your book, with use governed by your remaining classes.

During live testing: Try this on a disposable character before changing a valued build. Record your classes, ranks, and points, then zone and relog to check the result.

A useful start & room to growDeployed

New characters start at the tunnel with 100 platinum, a primary-class weapon, and a robe with 10 armor class and +5 to their main stat. These are creation provisions, not repeatable login rewards.

Polished, Exalted, and Ascended item tiers extend gear progression; Ascended pieces add heroic stats. Eligible summoned equipment becomes Ascended when a matching variant exists. Bags are available in sizes up to 40 slots, with base prices from 10 to 7,500 platinum. Merchant modifiers, specialty restrictions, and NO TRADE rules still matter.

Class courts & nearby servicesDeployed

All 16 class trainer-and-shop pairs are arranged into eight courts. Matching outfits help identify each pair. Twenty-two supply merchants and six crafting stations keep essentials close; overflow shops use numbered subtitles.

The Chronicler, bag seller, epic forge, restoration, blessing, and waypoint services share the hub. Shop stock, prices, interactions, and the newest layout remain under player testing.

A travel guide, rings & spiresDeployed

Hail Merrin in the tunnel’s travel alcove for ten city routes. Nearby return books bring you back, and the Plane of Knowledge connection remains available. Khalure offers Druid Rings; Khaleid offers eight Classic Wizard Spires destinations.

Merrin’s previously silent dialogue has been fixed and confirmed. Individual round trips, safe landings, and travel with pets still need testing. Destinations only appear when their travel checks allow them.

A way home & places to discoverDeployed

Find Ancient Attunement in the General abilities tab and make a hotkey named Nightcap Recall. It returns you to the tunnel with a five-second cast and ten-minute reuse, while keeping your existing bind and other home destinations intact.

Discover waypoint markers to unlock destinations through the waypoint service. Discovery, recall, and Explorer completion remain in player testing.

A market with a little lifeDeployed

Tunnel traders make finite buy and sell offers, with two offers per trader. Residents, walkers, and varied market chatter bring activity to the hub. City merchants can also receive limited finds.

Offers are finite, not an unlimited item source. Availability and prices are still being tested against the pace of player progression.

Level-appropriate blessingsDeployed

Hail Matron Restora for free movement, health, armor, and regeneration buffs suited to your level bracket. Return every ten levels for stronger versions.

Magus Goldwind offers the same package plus an experience blessing for 100 platinum. Read his dialogue before purchasing. Buff interactions and balance remain in live testing.

Private dungeon adventuresDeployed

Instance Keepers offer private versions of Befallen, Unrest, High Keep, Najena, Cazic-Thule, Permafrost, and Nagafen’s Lair. Hail the Keeper at the dungeon, then use the dialogue to enter or join your group leader.

Use #instances to list your open instances and remaining time. Keepers provide status and a confirmed reset option. Check the remaining time before a long run; entry, group joining, and reset behavior still need player verification.

Better payouts & bulk sellingDeployed

Merchant payouts for your loot are increased. The #sellall command sells eligible items in general inventory and bags without a merchant target, while keeping bags, NO TRADE, temporary, and worthless items.

Check your bags first: Bulk selling is immediate and is not a junk-only filter. Bank any sellable equipment or other valuables you want to keep before using it.

Advanced loot & saved choicesDeployed

Open /advloot for personal and shared loot, item inspection, corpse finding, and saved per-character filters. Choose Ask, Always loot, or Never; groups can use assignment, Need / Greed / Pass, and free grab. Optional coin splitting is included.

You must be close enough to collect. Bag space, loot rights, lore, and NO TRADE restrictions still apply; NO TRADE pickup asks for confirmation. Ordinary corpse looting remains available when advanced loot is disabled.

First try: Update through the usual starter, enter /advloot, enable Advanced loot and Apply filters, then test Ask on a common drop. The new window and shared-loot flows are in live testing.

Three pets, shared or individual ordersDeployed

Pet-summoning classes can each maintain a permanent pet, up to three across your held classes. The pet window shows your roster. Commands apply to all by default; select a pet by name or world target when you want to direct just one.

Enchanter animations now accept ordinary pet commands without an ability unlock, including when Enchanter is an additional class. Pet commands, zoning, and reconnect behavior remain under player testing.

Recoverable armor for each petDeployed

Inventory’s Pet Armor tab provides eight managed armor slots per permanent class pet. Select the pet, equip a compatible item from an otherwise empty cursor, inspect its loadout, or remove an item to recover it.

Stored armor remains available when the pet is inactive. Charm and swarm pets are excluded; some special item types cannot be stored. Transfers have combat, distance, and cursor checks. Test with inexpensive items while gameplay verification continues.

Beneficial buffs that stay with youDeployed

Player beneficial buffs do not expire from their normal duration countdown. Death removes them. Click-off, dispels, replacement and stacking, charge limits, and aura range still matter; debuffs and hostile NPC buffs keep their normal duration behavior.

This changes the strength of some combinations. Balance testing includes songs, disciplines, activated abilities, and defensive effects.

Smoother casting & meleeDeployed

Player damage-over-time spells cast instantly while retaining mana costs, target and resistance checks, and reuse limits. Ordinary direct-damage spells retain their casting behavior.

Primary and offhand autoattacks can continue during casting. Range, stun, fear, and other control restrictions still stop attacks as appropriate. A reported frozen personal casting bar remains under investigation.

No corpse run for your belongingsDeployed

Death keeps your equipment, inventory, and money with your character instead of leaving them on a corpse. You return to your bind point with your belongings.

Beneficial buffs are removed on death. This convenience does not promise the removal of every other death penalty or make a difficult encounter harmless.

One starter, automatic updatesDeployed

Use the same Nightcap shortcut each time. The starter updates the launcher and installs published game updates before Play. Your existing game folder stays in place.

The Nightcap interface loads automatically, with class labels, the ability Class column, and pet controls. Background interface requests no longer clutter local chat.

Get the latest fixes: Close the game, reopen your usual Nightcap starter, and let the update finish before selecting Play.

Classic models & Nightcap artworkDeployed

The managed client uses classic character and creature appearances where a supported older model exists, including original elemental colors. Creatures without an older counterpart keep their existing appearance.

Nightcap loading screens give the journey its own identity. The October 1 update fixes a startup problem introduced with the classic-model archives. Appearance and post-login checks continue.

04 / Before you head out

Good to know.

Bring curiosity.
Keep a little room in your bags.

Is Nightcap built for solo play?

Yes. Solo is the preferred balance target, and friends are welcome. Preparation, resource management, earned gear, and encounter execution should still matter. Convenience is not meant to make every fight effortless.

Where do I start?

New characters begin in the East Commonlands tunnel. Hail the Ancient Chronicler to choose extra classes, visit the class courts for training and spells, and speak to Merrin when you are ready to travel. The current progression era is Classic, with a level cap of 50.

How do I get these changes?

Close the game and reopen your usual Nightcap starter. Let updates complete, then use Play. Existing players keep their game folder. New players should get account and setup instructions directly from the server owner.

Does “deployed” mean finished?

It means the feature is installed on the hosted server or published through the patcher. Nightcap is in live testing: some behavior is supported by automated checks but still awaits player verification. The workbench lists undeployed candidates and unresolved work separately.

What should I include in a bug report?

Send the server owner your character’s classes and level, zone, approximate time, the action you tried, and what happened. Include the exact item, spell, ability, or NPC and a screenshot where useful. For missing items, loot, refunds, or coin, record before-and-after values and avoid repeatedly retrying the same operation.

Are new features automatically unlocked for everyone?

No. Level, held classes, individual ability requirements, item restrictions, and discovery still govern access. Spells must be learned and skills developed; travel conveniences do not remove progression requirements. Future releases and balance work may change the available content.
